Architectural Designer
Job Type: Full
Location: Bahadurgarh
Experience: 2-3 years

JOB DESCRIPTION:

We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented Architectural Designer with 2-3 years of professional experience to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate should be proficient in SketchUp, 3Ds Max, and presentation/working drawings, with a strong foundation in architectural and interior design. You will collaborate closely with our project team to create innovative and functional design solutions that meet client expectations and comply with building codes.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Prepare high-quality working drawings and presentation drawings using SketchUp, 3Ds Max, and other design tools.
  • Develop and contribute to architectural and interior design concepts for residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects.
  • Ensure all designs are compliant with building codes, regulations, and client specifications.
  • Coordinate and collaborate with internal teams and external stakeholders to ensure smooth project delivery.
  • Assist in creating detailed interior layouts, material specifications, and visualization outputs for client presentations.
  • Review and revise designs based on feedback to meet project goals and deadlines.

Key Skills & Qualifications:

  • 2-3 years of professional experience in architectural design and interior projects.
  • Proficiency in SketchUp, 3Ds Max, and working drawings.
  • Strong expertise in architectural and interior designing, including presentation drawings.
  • Good understanding of building codes and standards.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Excellent communication skills in English and Hindi.
  • Attention to detail, creativity, and a solution-oriented mindset.

Preferred Attributes:

  • Degree/Diploma in Architecture or Interior Design.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects efficiently and meet deadlines.
  • Passion for design innovation and staying updated with current industry trends

Role: Architect
